# Hidden Gem Brand System

## 1. Brand positioning

Hidden Gem is a social map app built around trust, place, and real-time connection. It should feel warm, intimate, and useful — like a private social layer over everyday life.

### Core idea
- People are not just on a map
- they are connected through what matters most: friends, places, moments

## 2. Brand voice

The voice should be:
- confident
- warm
- modern
- personal
- minimal but energetic

Examples:
- "See where your people are"
- "Save places worth sharing"
- "Keep the best spots for your friends"
- "Share what matters nearby"

## 3. Visual direction

### Style references
- Snapchat-inspired social simplicity
- clean mobile-first UI
- soft rounded cards and big product gestures
- minimal clutter with strong hierarchy

### Feel
- social and playful
- map-first but friend-first
- highly usable on mobile
- not too corporate

## 4. Brand palette

Primary colors:
- Yellow: #FFFC00
- Black: #111111
- White: #FFFFFF
- Warm gray: #F3F3F3
- Blue accent: #1E90FF
- Coral alert: #FF6C6C
- Green success: #5ACD4E

## 5. Typography

- Bold, geometric headings
- rounded sans-serif feel
- large type for key actions and status labels
- concise labels with uppercase accents for micro-copy

Recommended typography direction:
- bold black headings
- readable high-contrast body copy
- uppercase micro labels with tracking for status and metadata

## 6. Core UI patterns

- floating bottom navigation
- large product cards with rounded corners
- status pills for live, online, private, etc.
- minimal but expressive map overlays
- friend-first panels and quick actions

## 7. Screen inventory

### Primary screens
- onboarding
- login and signup
- profile creation
- friends list
- pending requests
- map screen
- friend detail
- gem detail
- spot detail
- direct chat
- notifications
- settings and privacy

### Common quick actions
- Share live location
- Add friend
- Create Gem
- Create Spot
- Send message
- Open map filters

## 8. Product copy examples

- "Share my location"
- "Friends only"
- "Public"
- "Create a Gem"
- "Add a Spot"
- "Hidden for your friends"
- "Seen by you and your circle"
- "Nearby friends"
- "Saved places"
- "New message"

## 9. Design principles

1. Make the map feel alive, not static.
2. Keep privacy visible and obvious.
3. Help people feel close to friends without clutter.
4. Make local discovery feel personal, not noisy.
5. Default to encouraging trust and convenience.

## 10. Brand promise

Hidden Gem is the place where your people, your places, and your location come together.

It helps users share what matters nearby without turning the experience into a public feed or a generic maps app.
